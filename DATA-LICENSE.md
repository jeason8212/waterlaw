https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The licence covers the query sets (`corpus/09`, `corpus/10`, `corpus/11`),
both annotators' raw judgements (`corpus/pre-adjudication/*.csv`,
`corpus/15_*.csv`), the shadow annotation, the adjudication record
(`corpus/17_*.md`), the synonym dictionary (`corpus/18_*.csv`), and the
evaluation outputs (`build/out/*.md`, `build/out/baseline_results.json`).

The statutory texts in `build/data/raw/*.txt` are official texts of the
People's Republic of China obtained from public official sources (National
Laws and Regulations Database, flk.npc.gov.cn, and ministry portals; see
`corpus/08_采集来源与入口.csv` for the source, version and retrieval date of
each instrument). They are reproduced here for research and verification; the
authoritative versions remain those published by the official databases.

The frozen graph database `build/out/kg.db` is a derived work from the above
and carries the same licence (CC BY 4.0).
